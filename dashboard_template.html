Playoff Odds Simulator

Monte Carlo simulation of the rest of the season. Each remaining week, every team's 8-category output is drawn from its own season mean/variance (from weekly data so far), matchups are randomized round-robin style, and category records are tallied. Repeats thousands of times to estimate each team's odds of finishing in a playoff spot. League is a 17-week regular season with 6 playoff spots — weeks remaining is auto-calculated from the latest week in the data, and the sim runs automatically with 2,000 trials.
